# Sales Dashboard — Revenue & Profitability Analysis

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze revenue, profitability, sales volume, customer orders, product categories, sales channels, quarters, and geographic performance.

The dashboard is designed to turn raw sales data into a concise management view, allowing users to identify **revenue trends, profitable markets, channel performance, and geographic concentration** through interactive filters and visualizations.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project was to build a business-focused sales dashboard that answers key questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit has the business generated?
- How is revenue and profit changing month over month?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Which sales channels contribute the most to overall revenue?
- How is revenue distributed across European regions?
- Which product categories and quarters are driving sales?
- Where are the strongest geographic markets?

The dashboard consolidates these metrics into a single interactive view so that business users can quickly monitor performance and identify areas requiring attention.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Profit

A monthly trend chart compares:

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit

This visualization makes it easier to identify seasonal patterns, monthly fluctuations, and periods of stronger or weaker financial performance.

The dashboard shows revenue reaching its highest level around **June–July**, while November also records a notable increase.

---

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue

The dashboard highlights the five highest-revenue countries:

1. **United Kingdom — ~$74K**
2. **Germany — ~$70K**
3. **France — ~$65K**
4. **Italy — ~$50K**
5. **Spain — ~$47K**

This view provides a quick understanding of the company's strongest national markets.

---

### 3. Revenue Share by Sales Channel

Revenue is segmented across four sales channels:

- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

The channel breakdown helps identify which distribution channels contribute most significantly to total revenue.

---

### 4. Revenue Share by Region

A regional donut chart provides a higher-level view of revenue distribution across:

- Central Europe
- Northern Europe
- Southern Europe
- Western Europe

This allows regional performance to be evaluated independently from individual countries.

---

### 5. Country-Wise Map

The geographic map provides a visual representation of sales activity across European markets.

This visualization can help identify:

- High-performing geographic areas
- Market concentration
- Potential regional expansion opportunities
- Differences between individual countries

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard's current view:

- The business generated approximately **$645K in total revenue** and **$296K in profit**.
- The **45.94% gross profit margin** indicates that a substantial portion of revenue remains after cost of goods sold.
- **June and July** appear to be among the strongest months for revenue generation.
- The **United Kingdom and Germany** are the strongest individual countries by revenue among the top five markets.
- Revenue is distributed across multiple sales channels rather than being dependent on a single channel.
- The geographic view indicates that sales are concentrated across several European markets, providing opportunities for country-level performance comparisons.
- The dashboard can be used to investigate whether strong revenue markets are also generating proportionally strong profit.

> **Note:** These observations represent the dashboard's current filtered view. Changing the slicer selections may change the underlying results and rankings.

## How to Use the Dashboard

1. Open the Excel workbook.
2. Start with the KPI cards to understand overall business performance.
3. Review the monthly revenue and profit trend.
4. Compare the top-performing countries.
5. Examine revenue distribution by sales channel and region.
6. Use the slicers to filter the dashboard.
7. Combine filters to perform more detailed analysis—for example:
   - Country + Product Category
   - Sales Channel + Quarter
   - Country + Sales Channel
   - Product Category + Quarter
8. Use the filtered results to investigate changes in revenue, profit, and sales volume.
